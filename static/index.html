WHAT ARE THE PURPLE CIRCLES / RINGS?
The pulsing purple dashed SVG halo rings over buildings represent LOW-CONFIDENCE AI DETECTIONS (< 0.65) from the ConvNeXt-Tiny satellite damage assessment model.

Why they exist: Following a hurricane or flood, satellite aerial imagery may be partially occluded by cloud shadow, tree canopy, or severe flood reflections, resulting in ambiguous damage predictions. When softmax classification confidence drops below 65% (calibrated via temperature scaling T = 1.28), the system flags the structure.

Tactical Action: Incident commanders and Urban Search & Rescue (USAR) units prioritize these purple beacons for close-up drone inspection (UAV RSC-01) or ground team verification before committing heavy logistics.
